Color Me Debt Free
Every debt becomes a picture. Every payment colors it in.
FinanceVisual

About
A debt payoff planner that turns every debt into a pixel picture. Add your credit cards, loans and pay-later plans, tell it what you can put toward debt each month, and see the month you'll be debt free with the snowball or avalanche method. Every payment you log colors in more tiles until the picture is finished and the debt is gone. A simple checklist shows exactly what to pay this month, a payday nudge helps you pay your plan first, and milestones and story-sized share cards celebrate every win. No bank login, no account, no ads, no tracking: your plan stays on your phone, with a private copy in your own iCloud.

What does "solo built" actually mean?

Every app was conceived, designed, coded, and shipped by one person. Every podcast was scripted, voiced, produced, and automated by one person. That includes game mechanics, App Store submissions, cloud infrastructure, content pipelines, and illustration direction across 491 unique images.

Modern AI tools made this possible, not by replacing thinking, but by compressing the gap between idea and execution. Every creative decision and quality judgment is human. The throughput is not.

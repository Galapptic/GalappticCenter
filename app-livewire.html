About
A minimalist electrical puzzle game where you rotate wire tiles to connect a power source to its destination. Handcrafted levels across five difficulty tiers, from Easy to Impossible. No timers, no ads, no pressure. Every level is procedurally generated and mathematically verified solvable before it ships.
Highlights
Five difficulty tiersFrom Easy to Impossible, with a harder circuit always waiting.
Zero pressureNo timers, no ads, no energy systems.
Multiple configurationsDifferent layouts so no two puzzles feel the same.
Verified solvableEvery level is mathematically confirmed before it ships.

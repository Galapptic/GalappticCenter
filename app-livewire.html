About
A clean, neon puzzle game: tap the wire tiles to rotate them and route power from the generator to the bulb. 10,207 levels across five difficulty tiers, from quick 4×3 warm-ups on Easy to sprawling 9×13 grids on Impossible. Every level has a par, the fewest taps it can be solved in: match it for three stars. No timers, no ads, no energy systems, and your progress is backed up to iCloud.

About
A minimalist electrical puzzle game where you rotate wire tiles to connect a power source to its destination. 442 handcrafted levels across five difficulty tiers, from Easy to Impossible. No timers, no ads, no pressure. Every single level is procedurally generated and mathematically verified solvable before it ships.

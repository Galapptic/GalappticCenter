About
A colorful spatial puzzle game where players drag overlapping 2x2 tile sections onto a grid to reconstruct a hidden color pattern. Gem economy, daily spin rewards, hint system, escalating levels, and colorblind mode included from day one.
Highlights
Colorblind modeFully accessible from launch.
Daily rewards16-slot animated spin wheel with gem economy.
Escalating difficultyMulti-level progression that grows with you.
Working prototypeFull drag-and-drop, conflict detection, and tutorial built.

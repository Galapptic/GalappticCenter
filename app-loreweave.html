About
A choose-your-own-adventure reading app with 60 branching stories across 13 distinct genre packs: noir detective fiction, Roman gladiator arenas, modern high school drama, and more. No energy systems, no timers. Just pure story. Packs unlock permanently for $2, $6, or $10.
Highlights
491 unique illustrationsEvery image was directed with a unique prompt tied to a specific story moment.
13 genre packsEach with its own distinct art style.
No subscriptionsOne-time pack unlocks. Fair pricing, forever.
You are the protagonistThe protagonist is never shown, so you imagine yourself in the story.

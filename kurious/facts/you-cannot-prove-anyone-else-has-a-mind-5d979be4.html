You Cannot Prove Anyone Else Has a Mind

The only consciousness you can verify exists is your own—everything else, including this sentence, might be an elaborate illusion.

Here's a problem philosophers have wrestled with for centuries: you experience your own thoughts directly, but you've never once experienced anyone else's. When your friend says they're happy, you see their smile and hear their words—but you cannot access their actual feeling. For all you can prove, they might be an empty automaton mimicking emotion, and you are the only conscious being in existence.

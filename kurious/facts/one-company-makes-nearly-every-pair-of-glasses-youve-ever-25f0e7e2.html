One Company Makes Nearly Every Pair of Glasses You've Ever Worn

Whether you buy Ray-Bans at Sunglass Hut or LensCrafters frames through your insurance, the money flows to the same Italian billionaire.

Walk into almost any eyewear store in America—LensCrafters, Pearle Vision, Sunglass Hut, Target Optical—and you're shopping at the same company. Pick up a pair of Ray-Bans, Oakleys, Persols, or even glasses branded Chanel, Prada, or Versace, and you're buying from that same company. File a claim through EyeMed vision insurance, and you're being reimbursed by that same company. Its name is Luxottica, and unless you've sought out an independent optician, it has almost certainly sold you your glasses.

Lesotho Is a Country Completely Swallowed by Another Country

Imagine a nation so completely surrounded by another that you literally cannot enter it without crossing through foreign territory first.

Lesotho holds a remarkable geographic distinction: it's one of only three countries in the world completely surrounded by a single other nation. Every inch of its border touches South Africa, making it a true enclave state. This tiny mountainous kingdom, roughly the size of Belgium, sits dramatically elevated on a plateau, with its lowest point being the highest of any country on Earth. Despite being geographically swallowed by its giant neighbor, Lesotho maintains complete sovereignty, its own monarchy, currency, and distinct cultural identity. The Basotho people have called these highlands home for centuries, fiercely preserving their independence through colonial pressures and apartheid-era tensions.

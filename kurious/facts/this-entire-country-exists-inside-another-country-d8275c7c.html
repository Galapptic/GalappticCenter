This Entire Country Exists Inside Another Country

Lesotho is the only independent nation on Earth completely surrounded by a single country—South Africa—making it one of just three enclaved countries worldwide.

Look at a map of southern Africa and you'll spot something strange: a hole in South Africa. That's Lesotho, a mountainous nation about the size of Belgium, entirely surrounded by its much larger neighbor. Every border Lesotho has—all 909 kilometers of it—touches only South Africa. To enter or leave by land, you must pass through South African territory. This makes Lesotho an 'enclaved country,' one of just three in the world alongside Vatican City and San Marino. But unlike those European microstates, Lesotho is a substantial nation with over two million people, its own monarchy, and a fierce sense of independence that kept it from being absorbed during colonialism.

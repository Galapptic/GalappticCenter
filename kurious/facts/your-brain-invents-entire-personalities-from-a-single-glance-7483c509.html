Your brain invents entire personalities from a single glance

That stranger across the room? Your brain has already decided their life story before they've said a word.

You see someone for the first time and immediately know things about them. Or at least you think you do. She's confident. He's untrustworthy. They're creative. Your brain assembles these rich personality profiles in a fraction of a second, drawing wildly elaborate conclusions from nothing more than a face, a posture, or a piece of clothing. It happens so fast and feels so natural that you barely notice you're doing it. But you are. Every single time you encounter a stranger, your mind becomes a fiction writer, spinning complete character studies from the thinnest possible evidence.

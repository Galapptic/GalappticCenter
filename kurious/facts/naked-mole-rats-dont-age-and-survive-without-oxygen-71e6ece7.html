Naked mole rats don't age and survive without oxygen

One bizarre mammal ignores aging, laughs at cancer, and can live without breathing for nearly twenty minutes.

Naked mole rats are the weirdest mammals on Earth. These wrinkly, nearly blind creatures live in underground colonies like ants, complete with a queen. But here's what really sets them apart: they don't seem to age. While a typical mouse lives three years and shows all the signs of getting old, naked mole rats can live past thirty with barely any decline. Their risk of death doesn't increase with age like every other mammal. They rarely get cancer. And if you cut off their oxygen supply, they just switch to a backup metabolic system that keeps them alive for eighteen minutes. They're basically biological rule breakers.

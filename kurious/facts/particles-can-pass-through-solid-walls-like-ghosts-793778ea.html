Particles Can Pass Through Solid Walls Like Ghosts

Quantum mechanics allows particles to teleport through barriers they physically cannot climb over, and this impossible-sounding trick powers the Sun itself.

Imagine throwing a ball at a wall and watching it appear on the other side without breaking through. Absurd, right? Yet subatomic particles do exactly this constantly. Quantum tunneling allows electrons, protons, and other particles to pass through energy barriers they classically shouldn't have enough energy to overcome. It's not that they break through or go around—they literally appear on the other side as if the barrier barely existed. This isn't science fiction; it's fundamental physics happening trillions of times per second inside your body right now. The phenomenon emerges from particles behaving as probability waves rather than solid objects, allowing a small chance of existing beyond seemingly impenetrable walls.

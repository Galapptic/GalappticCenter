This Metal Literally Melts in the Palm of Your Hand

Gallium sits perfectly solid on your desk, but the moment you pick it up, it transforms into a silvery puddle right in your palm.

Gallium is a peculiar element with a melting point of just 29.76°C (85.6°F)—comfortably below human body temperature of 37°C but above typical room temperature. This means a chunk of gallium appears completely solid and metallic sitting on a table, yet transforms into a shimmering liquid puddle within seconds of touching your skin. The sensation is surreal: you're literally watching metal melt from nothing but your body heat. Unlike mercury, gallium is non-toxic, making it safe to handle. This dramatic demonstration has made gallium a favorite among science educators and YouTube experimenters who love watching people's disbelief when solid metal liquefies before their eyes.

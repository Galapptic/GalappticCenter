Why Some Cultures Count to Twenty on Their Fingers and Toes

The Mayans built one of history's most accurate calendars using a number system based on something you're wearing right now: sandals.

Most of the world counts in tens. We have ten digits on our hands, after all—the word "digit" itself means both finger and numeral. But scattered across the globe, from the rainforests of Mexico to the hills of France, people developed a different system. They counted in twenties, using every finger and every toe. The technical term is vigesimal, from the Latin word for twenty. And it left marks on languages that billions of people still speak today.

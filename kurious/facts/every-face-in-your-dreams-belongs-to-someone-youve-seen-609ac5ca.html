Every face in your dreams belongs to someone you've seen

That menacing stranger from last night's nightmare? You've definitely met them before, even if just for a second.

Think about the last dream you had with people in it. Maybe there was a threatening figure, a helpful guide, or just random passersby populating your dreamscape. Here's the unsettling part: your brain didn't invent any of those faces. It pulled them from memory. Every single person you've ever dreamed about is someone whose face you've actually encountered, even if only briefly. Your mind can't generate a truly novel human face from scratch. It can only remix and recall. That creepy dream villain? Probably the barista you saw once three years ago.

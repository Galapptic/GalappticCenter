Your brain fakes multitasking by switching attention incredibly fast

That feeling of doing two things at once? Your brain is actually lying to you.

You think you're reading this while listening to music, maybe with a conversation happening nearby. It feels seamless, like your brain handles it all simultaneously. But here's the truth: your brain can't actually process multiple streams of conscious thought at once. What feels like multitasking is really your attention switching back and forth so quickly that you don't notice the gaps. It's like a spotlight that can only shine on one thing at a time, but moves so fast it seems to illuminate everything at once. Your brain is incredible at creating this illusion, but it's still just an illusion.

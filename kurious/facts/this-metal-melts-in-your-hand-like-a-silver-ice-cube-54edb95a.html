This Metal Melts in Your Hand Like a Silver Ice Cube

Hold a chunk of gallium in your palm and watch it transform into a shimmering liquid puddle in less than a minute.

Gallium is one of the strangest elements on the periodic table. This silvery metal has a melting point of just 29.76°C (85.57°F)—slightly below human body temperature of 37°C. Pick up a solid chunk of gallium, and your body heat alone will cause it to liquefy in your palm, flowing like mercury but completely safe to touch. Unlike most metals that require furnaces to melt, gallium surrenders to the warmth of a summer day. It's dense, shiny, and behaves exactly like you'd expect metal to behave—until it doesn't. The experience is surreal: watching something so obviously metallic simply dissolve into liquid from nothing but the heat of your skin.

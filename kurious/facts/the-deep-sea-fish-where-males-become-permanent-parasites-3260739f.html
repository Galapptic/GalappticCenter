The Deep-Sea Fish Where Males Become Permanent Parasites

In the crushing darkness of the deep ocean, male anglerfish bite onto females and never let go—their bodies literally fusing together until the male becomes nothing more than a sperm-producing appendage.

Two thousand meters below the surface, where sunlight never reaches and the pressure would crush a submarine, finding a mate presents an almost impossible challenge. The deep ocean is vast, dark, and sparsely populated. Most fish that live here go their entire lives without encountering another member of their species. Anglerfish solved this problem in the most radical way evolution has ever devised: when a male finds a female, he bites into her flesh and never lets go. His body fuses with hers. His eyes degenerate. His organs dissolve. He becomes, quite literally, a part of her.

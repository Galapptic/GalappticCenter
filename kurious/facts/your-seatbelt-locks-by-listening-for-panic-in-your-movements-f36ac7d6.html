Your seatbelt locks by listening for panic in your movements

That click when you yank your seatbelt isn't a mistake. It's detecting the exact signature of danger.

Pull your seatbelt slowly and it glides out smoothly. Yank it fast and it locks solid. This isn't about speed alone. The mechanism inside is actually sensing acceleration, the sudden change in how fast you're moving. It's the automotive equivalent of knowing the difference between someone calmly reaching for your shoulder and someone grabbing you in alarm. Your seatbelt can tell when your body is moving with the controlled intention of leaning forward versus the violent jerk of a collision. That distinction keeps you safe while letting you move naturally.

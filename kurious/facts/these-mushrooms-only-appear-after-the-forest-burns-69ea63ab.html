These Mushrooms Only Appear After the Forest Burns

Foragers know that the best time to hunt for prized morel mushrooms is the spring after a forest fire turns the landscape to ash.

Most fungi flee fire. Morchella mushrooms chase it. These honeycomb-capped delicacies, better known as morels, fruit in astonishing abundance on recently scorched ground—sometimes emerging by the thousands in a single burned acre. Professional foragers track wildfire maps the way stockbrokers watch market tickers.

The Only Liquid Element That Eats Through Glass

Bromine is so viciously corrosive that it can dissolve glass, attacks most metals, and burns human flesh on contact—yet it's hiding in your swimming pool and your camera film.

There are only two elements on the periodic table that exist as liquids at room temperature. One is mercury, the silvery stuff in old thermometers. The other is bromine—a fuming, red-brown liquid so aggressive it dissolves glass, corrodes platinum, and can eat through human tissue in seconds. It's one of the most dangerous substances routinely handled in chemistry labs, and storing it requires special containers lined with wax, Teflon, or lead because ordinary glass simply won't survive.

This Mathematical Shape Contains Infinite Copies of Itself Forever

Zoom into the Mandelbrot Set's boundary and you'll find perfect miniature versions of the entire set hiding everywhere, each containing infinite copies of their own.

The Mandelbrot Set is mathematics' most famous fractal, a shape of infinite complexity generated by an absurdly simple equation. But here's what makes it truly mind-bending: zoom into its intricate boundary, and you'll discover perfect miniature copies of the entire set embedded within it. Each of these mini-Mandelbrots contains its own infinite copies, which contain more copies, and so on forever. It's like finding a photograph of your entire universe tucked into every corner of reality. Unlike simple self-similar shapes like triangles within triangles, these copies aren't immediately obvious. They hide in the swirling chaos of the boundary, connected by impossibly thin tendrils, waiting to be discovered at every scale of magnification.

The God Chained Beneath a Serpent's Dripping Fangs

For killing a beloved god, Loki was bound beneath a venomous snake—but the Norse knew his chains wouldn't hold forever.

Deep beneath the earth, in a cave far from Asgard's golden halls, Loki lies bound across three sharp stones. Above him hangs a serpent, its fangs dripping venom toward his face. His wife Sigyn stands beside him, holding a bowl to catch the poison. But when the bowl fills and she turns away to empty it, the venom strikes his skin. His convulsions shake the world—what mortals call earthquakes.

This is Loki's punishment for the worst of his crimes: engineering the death of Baldr, the radiant god whom nearly everything in existence had sworn never to harm. The gods' vengeance was brutal and permanent. Or so they thought.

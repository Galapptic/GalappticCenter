Prometheus Stole Divine Fire and Suffered Forever for Humanity

A Titan was chained to a mountain where an eagle devoured his liver daily for eternity—all because he gave humans the gift of fire.

In Greek mythology, Prometheus wasn't just any troublemaker—he was a Titan who dared to steal from Zeus himself. The gods jealously guarded fire, keeping humanity cold, vulnerable, and powerless in the dark. Prometheus felt compassion for these struggling mortals and devised a bold plan. He crept up to Mount Olympus, stole sacred fire from the gods' hearth, and smuggled it down to Earth hidden in a hollow fennel stalk. This single act transformed human civilization, giving us warmth, cooking, metalworking, and protection from predators. But Zeus was furious. No one defied the king of gods without consequences, and Prometheus would learn exactly how creative divine punishment could be.

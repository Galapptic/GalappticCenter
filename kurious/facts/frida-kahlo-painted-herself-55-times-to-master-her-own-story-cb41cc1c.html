Frida Kahlo Painted Herself 55 Times to Master Her Own Story

While most artists paint landscapes or lovers, Frida Kahlo spent her career staring into a mirror and transforming her pain into 55 unflinching self-portraits.

Frida Kahlo created around 200 paintings in her lifetime, and 55 of them featured the same subject: herself. This wasn't vanity—it was necessity and defiance. After a devastating bus accident at age eighteen left her bedridden for months, her mother installed a mirror above her bed and gave her painting supplies. Kahlo later explained, 'I paint myself because I am so often alone and because I am the subject I know best.' Each self-portrait became a visual diary, documenting her physical suffering, turbulent marriage to muralist Diego Rivera, and fierce Mexican identity. Her distinctive unibrow and traditional Tehuana dresses became iconic symbols of artistic authenticity.

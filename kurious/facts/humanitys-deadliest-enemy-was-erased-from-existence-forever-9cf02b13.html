Humanity's Deadliest Enemy Was Erased From Existence Forever

A disease that killed more people than both World Wars combined became the first—and still only—human disease deliberately wiped off the face of the Earth.

Smallpox was humanity's longest nightmare. In the 20th century alone, it killed an estimated 300 million people—more than every war of that century combined. The virus caused fever, then distinctive fluid-filled pustules covering the entire body. Survivors were often left blind or horribly scarred. It killed pharaohs, emperors, and peasants alike for thousands of years. Then something remarkable happened. Through a coordinated global vaccination campaign led by the World Health Organization, smallpox was systematically hunted down country by country. The last natural case occurred in Somalia in 1977. By 1980, WHO officially declared smallpox eradicated. It remains the only human disease ever deliberately eliminated from nature.

Your brain can juggle only four thoughts at once

The difference between remembering a phone number and forgetting it mid-dial comes down to a tiny bottleneck.

Try to remember this list: apple, chair, cloud, piano, rocket, mirror, elephant. Now look away. How many could you hold? If you're like most people, you hit a wall around four items. Your working memory is like a mental juggler, keeping thoughts airborne while you use them. But unlike a computer's RAM that can expand, your brain's juggling capacity tops out surprisingly fast. This isn't about long term storage. It's about the thoughts you're actively thinking right now, the mental workspace where you solve problems and make decisions.

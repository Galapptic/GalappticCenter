The Goddess Who Rules the Arctic Dead From the Ocean Floor

When Inuit hunters lost a loved one at sea, they believed the drowned didn't vanish—they descended to serve a fingerless goddess in an underwater kingdom beneath the ice.

In the freezing waters of the Arctic, where survival depends on the hunt for seals and whales, the Inuit developed a spiritual world shaped by the sea itself. At its center sits Sedna, a goddess who dwells at the bottom of the ocean, surrounded by all the marine animals that sustain human life—and all the souls of those who drowned. She is both provider and punisher, and reaching her requires a journey that only the most powerful shamans could survive.

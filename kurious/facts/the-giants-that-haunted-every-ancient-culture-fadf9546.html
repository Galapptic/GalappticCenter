The Giants That Haunted Every Ancient Culture

From Mesopotamia to Mesoamerica, civilizations that never contacted each other all recorded the same impossible beings: towering hybrids born when gods mated with humans.

The Hebrew Bible calls them Nephilim. The Greeks called them Gigantes. The Sumerians wrote of the Anunnaki's monstrous offspring. Norse legends spoke of the Jötnar. Across oceans, the Maya recorded giants who built the first world, and the Inca described a race of enormous beings who angered the gods. These weren't borrowed stories—many of these cultures had no contact with each other. Yet they all documented the same phenomenon: giants born from the union of divine and human.

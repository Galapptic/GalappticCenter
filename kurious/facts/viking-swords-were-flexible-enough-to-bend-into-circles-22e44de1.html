Viking swords were flexible enough to bend into circles

The deadliest warriors of medieval Europe carried swords that could bend completely in half without breaking.

Viking sagas describe warriors bending their swords back into shape with their feet mid-battle. This sounds like pure myth until you examine actual Viking Age swords in museums today. Many can flex dramatically without snapping. Some experimental archaeologists have even reproduced swords that bend into near circles and spring back straight. This wasn't a design flaw. It was intentional engineering that reveals how Vikings thought about combat differently than we might imagine. A blade that bends absorbs impact energy that would otherwise shatter it. The flexibility kept warriors alive.

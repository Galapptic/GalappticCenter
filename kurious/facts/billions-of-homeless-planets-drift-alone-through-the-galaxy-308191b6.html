Billions of Homeless Planets Drift Alone Through the Galaxy

For every star in the Milky Way, there may be at least one planet wandering through interstellar space with no sun to orbit.

Not every planet gets to keep its star. In the chaos of young solar systems, gravitational tug-of-war between giant planets can hurl smaller worlds into the void. These rogue planets—also called free-floating planets—then drift through interstellar space, untethered to any sun, for billions of years.

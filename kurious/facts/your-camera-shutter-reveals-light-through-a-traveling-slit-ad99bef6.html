Your camera shutter reveals light through a traveling slit

Every photo you've ever taken was captured by a tiny gap racing across your sensor at incredible speed.

When you press the shutter button on most modern cameras, you might imagine a door that opens and closes to let light in. But that's not what happens at all. Instead, two curtains race across your camera sensor one after the other, creating a narrow slit between them. This slit sweeps across the entire frame like a scanner moving across a document. Your photo isn't captured all at once. It's painted onto the sensor bit by bit as this traveling window of light moves from one side to the other. The entire journey takes just a fraction of a second, but that's enough time for some very weird things to happen.

You Cannot Prove Anyone Else Has a Mind

Every person you've ever loved might be a philosophical zombie—a perfect imitation of consciousness with nothing inside.

Here's a thought that might keep you up tonight: you have absolutely no way to prove that anyone else is conscious. Sure, people act conscious—they laugh, cry, claim to feel pain. But how do you know there's actually something it feels like to be them? You only have direct access to one mind: your own. Everyone else could theoretically be an elaborate automaton, responding to stimuli without any inner experience whatsoever. This isn't paranoid delusion—it's a legitimate philosophical puzzle that has stumped thinkers for centuries. We call it the Problem of Other Minds, and it remains genuinely unsolved.

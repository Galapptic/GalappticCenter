Vampire finches drink blood from living seabirds without killing them

On a remote Galápagos island, tiny songbirds have become vampires, feeding on blood from birds fifty times their size.

The vampire finch lives only on Wolf and Darwin islands in the Galápagos. This small bird, closely related to the famous Darwin's finches, does something almost no other bird does. It pecks at the wing bases of massive seabirds like boobies and nazca frigatebirds until it draws blood, then drinks it. The seabirds barely react, often sitting still while the finches feed. These vampire finches don't kill their hosts. They treat them more like living juice boxes, taking a meal and flying off. It's one of nature's strangest examples of dietary evolution.

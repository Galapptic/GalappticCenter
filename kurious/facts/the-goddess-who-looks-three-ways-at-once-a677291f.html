The Goddess Who Looks Three Ways at Once

At every crossroads in ancient Greece, a three-faced goddess stood watching—and travelers left her dinner at midnight to keep her happy.

Where roads meet, decisions happen. Ancient Greeks believed these liminal spaces—thresholds between here and there, this choice and that—belonged to a goddess who could see all paths simultaneously. Hecate didn't just guard crossroads. She embodied the strange power of in-between places, standing in three forms that faced backward into the past, forward into the future, and sideways into realms mortals couldn't perceive.

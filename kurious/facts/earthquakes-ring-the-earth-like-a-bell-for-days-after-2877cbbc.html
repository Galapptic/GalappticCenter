Earthquakes ring the Earth like a bell for days after striking

Every massive earthquake sets the entire planet humming with vibrations that continue for weeks, imperceptible to us but measurable by instruments.

When a major earthquake strikes, it doesn't just shake the ground nearby. The entire Earth rings like a bell that's been struck by a hammer. These vibrations ripple through the planet for days or even weeks afterward, long after we've stopped feeling any tremors. Sensitive instruments called seismometers can detect these persistent oscillations anywhere on Earth, no matter where the original quake occurred. The planet literally vibrates at specific frequencies, humming at pitches far too low for human ears to hear. It's as if Earth itself is a musical instrument that remembers being played.

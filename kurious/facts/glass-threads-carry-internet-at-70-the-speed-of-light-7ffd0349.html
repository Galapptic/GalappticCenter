Glass Threads Carry Internet at 70% the Speed of Light

While your brain's electrical signals crawl at 270 mph, fiber optic pulses race through glass at 450 million mph—making your thoughts literally slower than your internet connection.

Fiber optic cables are humanity's elegant solution to moving massive amounts of data. These hair-thin strands of ultra-pure glass trap light inside through total internal reflection—photons literally bounce along the fiber's walls like a perfectly aimed billiard ball. Each pulse of light represents data: on means one, off means zero. A single fiber can carry trillions of these pulses per second. The light travels at roughly 200,000 kilometers per second—about 70% of light's maximum speed in vacuum. Compare that to electrical signals in copper wire, which max out around 200,000 km/s but degrade rapidly. Your neural signals? A sluggish 120 meters per second. The internet quite literally thinks faster than you do.

The Three-Year Wait Behind Every Drop of Vanilla

The world's most popular flavor comes from an orchid so finicky that nearly every pod on Earth exists because a human touched it at exactly the right moment.

Vanilla is the only orchid grown commercially for food. The vine, Vanilla planifolia, produces pale yellow-green flowers that bloom for just 24 hours. Miss that window, and the flower drops—no pod, no vanilla. In the wild, only specific bees and hummingbirds native to Mexico can pollinate it. Everywhere else, humans must do the job by hand.

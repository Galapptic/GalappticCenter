This Entire Country Exists Inside Another Country

Lesotho is one of only three countries on Earth completely surrounded by a single nation, creating a geographic island without any coastline or alternative neighbors.

Imagine needing to cross through another country just to reach anyone else in the world. That's daily reality for Lesotho, a small mountainous nation entirely encircled by South Africa. No matter which direction you travel from Lesotho, you'll end up in South Africa—there's simply no other option. This geographic quirk makes Lesotho an enclave, a territory completely surrounded by another country's borders. About the size of Belgium, Lesotho sits high in the Drakensberg and Maluti mountain ranges, earning its nickname 'The Kingdom in the Sky.' Its lowest point is higher than any other country's lowest point on Earth.

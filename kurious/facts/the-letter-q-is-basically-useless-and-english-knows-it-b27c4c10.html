The Letter Q Is Basically Useless and English Knows It

Every other letter in the English alphabet can stand alone, but Q is so dependent on U that linguists consider it the most redundant character we still bother to type.

Open any English dictionary and you'll find Q pulling the same trick over and over: queen, quick, quilt, quote. In virtually every native English word, Q appears joined at the hip with U, and together they make a sound that K and W handle just fine on their own. So why do we keep Q around at all?

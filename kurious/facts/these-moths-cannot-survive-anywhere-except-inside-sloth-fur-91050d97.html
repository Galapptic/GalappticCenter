These Moths Cannot Survive Anywhere Except Inside Sloth Fur

A species of moth has evolved to live its entire adult life exclusively in the coarse, algae-covered fur of wild sloths—the only habitat on Earth where it can survive.

Sloths are famously slow, but their fur is surprisingly busy. Living among those coarse hairs are specialized moths—species from the genus Cryptoses—that exist nowhere else on the planet. These sloth moths spend their entire adult lives nestled in sloth fur, feeding on secretions and algae growing on the animal's coat. The relationship is so exclusive that if a sloth dies, so do its moth passengers. Scientists have counted over 100 moths living on a single sloth. The moths have evolved flattened bodies and strong claws specifically to grip sloth hair and resist being groomed away. It's one of nature's strangest addresses.

Fermentation Turns Humble Cabbage Into Billions of Living Microbes

A single cup of sauerkraut contains more beneficial bacteria than an entire bottle of probiotic supplements.

When you pack shredded cabbage with salt and leave it alone, something magical happens. The salt draws out liquid and creates an environment where beneficial Lactobacillus bacteria—already present on cabbage leaves—begin feasting on natural sugars. Over days and weeks, these microbes multiply into the billions, producing lactic acid that preserves the vegetable and creates that distinctive tangy flavor. The result is sauerkraut, a living food teeming with probiotics. Unlike pasteurized products, raw fermented cabbage delivers these bacteria directly to your gut, where they support digestion and immune function. This ancient preservation technique transforms a cheap, humble vegetable into a nutritional powerhouse that humans have relied upon for thousands of years.

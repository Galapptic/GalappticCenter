The Dome of the Rock Hides Its Weight in Plain Sight

Jerusalem's golden dome looks like it floats, and that is because of a carefully planned pattern of piers, columns, and light, plus a secret about what the dome is made of.

Completed around 691 CE under the Umayyad caliph Abd al-Malik, the Dome of the Rock is the oldest surviving major monument of Islamic architecture. Its gilded dome spans about 66 feet (20 m) and rises roughly 115 feet (35 m) above the Temple Mount. Yet from inside, it doesn't seem to press down at all.

The Dead Sea Is Dying and Sinking Deeper Every Year

Earth's lowest land point drops another meter annually while its famous salt lake literally disappears before our eyes.

The Dead Sea sits at roughly 430 meters below sea level, making it the lowest point on any continental surface on Earth. But here's the alarming part: it's getting lower. The water level drops by about one meter every year, and the shoreline has retreated dramatically since the 1960s. This hypersaline lake, nestled between Jordan, Israel, and the West Bank, contains water nearly ten times saltier than typical ocean water. That extreme salinity creates the famous floating experience tourists love. But the same waters that have attracted visitors for millennia are now shrinking at an unprecedented rate, leaving behind sinkholes and stranded resorts.

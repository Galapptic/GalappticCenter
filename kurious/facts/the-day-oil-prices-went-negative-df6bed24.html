The Day Oil Prices Went Negative

On April 20, 2020, traders paid buyers up to $40 a barrel just to take crude oil off their hands.

Commodity prices aren't supposed to go below zero. If nobody wants your product, you can always just... keep it. But oil doesn't work that way. On April 20, 2020, the price of West Texas Intermediate crude for May delivery plunged to negative $37.63 per barrel. Sellers were paying buyers to take the stuff away.

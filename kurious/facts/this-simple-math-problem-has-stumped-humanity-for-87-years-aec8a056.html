This Simple Math Problem Has Stumped Humanity for 87 Years

Pick any number, apply two dead-simple rules, and you'll always reach 1—except nobody can prove why.

Here's a game anyone can play: pick any positive whole number. If it's even, divide by two. If it's odd, multiply by three and add one. Repeat. Mathematicians have tested this on numbers up to 2^68—that's billions of billions—and every single one eventually spirals down to 1. Then it loops: 1, 4, 2, 1, forever. The Collatz Conjecture claims this happens for every number. Sounds obvious, right? But here's the twist: despite its kindergarten-level rules, no mathematician on Earth has ever proven it true. Paul Erdős, one of history's greatest mathematicians, declared that mathematics is simply 'not ready for such problems.'

Massive rivers flow beneath the ocean for thousands of miles

The largest river on Earth isn't the Amazon. It's invisible, flowing along the bottom of the Black Sea.

Deep beneath the ocean surface, massive rivers flow along the seafloor just like rivers on land. These underwater channels have banks, levees, and floodplains. They carry sediment and saltwater thousands of miles across the abyssal plains. The largest one ever measured runs along the bottom of the Black Sea. If it flowed on land, it would be one of the six largest rivers on the planet. These hidden waterways can stretch for hundreds of miles and reach depths equivalent to the height of skyscrapers. They're completely invisible from above, yet they're reshaping the ocean floor right now.

These finches peck seabirds until they bleed, then drink

On a remote island, Darwin's finches evolved a taste for blood and regularly feed on living birds.

The vampire finch lives on Wolf and Darwin islands in the Galápagos, two of the most isolated specks of land on Earth. This small bird has developed an extraordinary feeding habit. It lands on the backs of nesting seabirds like boobies and nazca birds, then uses its sharp beak to peck at the base of their wing feathers. The pecking creates small wounds that bleed. The finch drinks the fresh blood directly from the living bird. The seabirds barely react, often sitting still while being fed upon. This isn't occasional behavior. Blood has become a regular part of the vampire finch's diet.

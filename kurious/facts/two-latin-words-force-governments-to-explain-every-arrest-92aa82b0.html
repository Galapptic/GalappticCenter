Two Latin Words Force Governments to Explain Every Arrest

A legal phrase invented 800 years ago remains the single most powerful weapon against tyrannical imprisonment anywhere on Earth.

Habeas corpus literally means 'you shall have the body' in Latin. It's a court order demanding that authorities physically bring a prisoner before a judge and legally justify why they're being held. No secret dungeons, no indefinite detention without explanation. If the government can't provide a valid reason, the person walks free. This principle forms the bedrock of legal protection against arbitrary imprisonment. It doesn't determine guilt or innocence—it simply ensures that no one can be locked away and forgotten. Every democracy considers it fundamental, and its suspension is often the first sign of authoritarianism taking hold.

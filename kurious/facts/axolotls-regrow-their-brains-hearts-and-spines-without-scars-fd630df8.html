Axolotls regrow their brains, hearts, and spines without scars

While you're stuck with the body you've got, one animal can regrow its brain as easily as you grow fingernails.

The axolotl looks like a creature that couldn't decide whether to be a tadpole or a dragon. These Mexican salamanders spend their entire lives underwater with feathery gills sprouting from their heads. But their real superpower isn't their permanent childhood. It's their ability to regrow almost anything. Lose a leg? It grows back in weeks, complete with bones, muscles, and nerves in exactly the right places. Damage your heart? The axolotl rebuilds it without a scar. Even a destroyed section of brain or severed spinal cord regenerates perfectly. They can do this over and over, throughout their entire lives.

Hagfish produce buckets of slime in seconds using thread grenades

A single hagfish can turn a bucket of water into thick slime faster than you can tie your shoes.

Hagfish are eel-like creatures that have mastered the strangest defense mechanism in the ocean. When a predator attacks, they release a tiny amount of milky goo into the water. Within half a second, that goo explodes into up to five gallons of thick, fibrous slime. The slime clogs the gills of any fish trying to eat them, causing attackers to gag and retreat. It's so effective that hagfish rarely get eaten despite being slow, nearly blind, and spending their days burrowing in mud eating dead things on the seafloor. They've survived virtually unchanged for 300 million years.

Magnetars Spin So Fast They Literally Twist Space Around Them

Rotating up to 700 times per second with magnetic fields a quadrillion times stronger than Earth's, magnetars drag the fabric of spacetime into spiraling invisible funnels.

Magnetars are the universe's most extreme magnets—neutron stars with magnetic fields so powerful they could erase your credit card from halfway across the solar system. These stellar corpses pack more mass than our Sun into a sphere just 20 kilometers wide, then spin at mind-bending speeds. Some complete hundreds of rotations every second. At these velocities, Einstein's general relativity kicks in hard. The magnetar's immense mass, combined with its rotation, actually drags spacetime along with it like honey swirling around a spinning spoon. This effect creates invisible funnel-shaped distortions extending outward from the star's poles—regions where space itself is twisted into corkscrew patterns.

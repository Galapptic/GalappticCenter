Tardigrades turn themselves into glass to survive the impossible

A microscopic animal can survive boiling, freezing, and the vacuum of space by transforming its insides into glass.

Tardigrades are eight-legged microscopic animals that look like chubby bears under a microscope. They live everywhere, from mountaintops to ocean trenches, and they possess a superpower that seems ripped from science fiction. When conditions turn deadly, tardigrades can survive temperatures near absolute zero, heat past the boiling point of water, crushing pressures, lethal radiation, and even the vacuum of space. They've been around for over half a billion years, outlasting most mass extinctions. The secret to their resilience is bizarre. When threatened, they essentially replace the water inside their cells with biological glass.

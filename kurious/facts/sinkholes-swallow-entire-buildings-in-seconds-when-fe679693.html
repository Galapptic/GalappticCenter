Sinkholes swallow entire buildings in seconds when limestone caves collapse

In some cities, the earth can suddenly open up and gulp down houses, cars, and people without warning.

Sinkholes are exactly what they sound like. The ground simply opens up and everything on the surface drops into a void below. A car disappears into a suburban street. A house tilts and vanishes into the earth. These aren't rare events in disaster movies. They happen regularly in places like Florida, China, and Guatemala. The 2013 sinkhole that swallowed a sleeping man in his Florida bedroom made headlines precisely because it could happen to anyone. One moment solid ground exists beneath you. The next moment it doesn't. The earth has been hollow all along.

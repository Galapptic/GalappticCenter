The Explosive Chemistry That Saves Your Life in 40 Milliseconds

Every car you've ever ridden in contains enough explosive compound to detonate with the force of a small bomb—and that's exactly what keeps you alive in a crash.

When your car slams into something at highway speed, you have about 40 milliseconds before your skull meets the steering wheel. That's roughly half the time it takes to blink. In that sliver of a moment, a small metal canister packed with sodium azide—a compound toxic enough that a single gram can kill an adult—detonates with precisely controlled violence, flooding a nylon bag with nitrogen gas fast enough to catch your falling body.

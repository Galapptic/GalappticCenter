Axolotls Regrow Perfect Limbs Complete With Bones and Nerves

While humans form scar tissue after injuries, axolotls can regenerate entire limbs, spinal cords, and even parts of their hearts and brains with zero scarring.

If an axolotl loses a leg, it doesn't panic—it just grows a new one. These remarkable amphibians, native to lakes near Mexico City, possess perhaps the most impressive healing ability in the animal kingdom. Within weeks of losing a limb, they regenerate everything: bones, muscles, blood vessels, and nerves, all perfectly positioned and fully functional. But it doesn't stop there. Axolotls can also regenerate portions of their spinal cord, heart tissue, and even parts of their brain. Most incredibly, they do this without any scarring whatsoever. The new limb is indistinguishable from the original, functioning perfectly throughout their lives. This superpower has made them scientific celebrities.

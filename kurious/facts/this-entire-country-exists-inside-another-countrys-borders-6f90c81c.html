This Entire Country Exists Inside Another Country's Borders

Lesotho is one of only three countries on Earth completely surrounded by a single nation, and it's the only one located entirely above 1,000 meters elevation.

Imagine a country so thoroughly enclosed that you literally cannot reach it without passing through another nation first. That's Lesotho, a small kingdom about the size of Belgium, completely surrounded by South Africa. This makes it an enclaved nation—landlocked on all sides by a single country. But geography gets even more dramatic here. Lesotho sits entirely within the Drakensberg and Maluti mountain ranges, with its lowest point at 1,400 meters—the highest low point of any country on Earth. The Basotho people call their homeland the 'Kingdom in the Sky,' and when you realize the entire nation is higher than Denver, Colorado, that nickname makes perfect sense.

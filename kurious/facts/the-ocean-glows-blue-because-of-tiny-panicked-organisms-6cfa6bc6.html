The Ocean Glows Blue Because of Tiny Panicked Organisms

When waves crash into certain beaches at night, single-celled creatures respond to the disturbance by flooding the water with cold blue light.

Dinoflagellates are microscopic plankton that drift through oceans worldwide, but some species carry an unusual defense mechanism. When disturbed—by a breaking wave, a swimming fish, or a kayaker's paddle—they emit a flash of blue-green light. One cell produces only a faint spark. Millions together turn the surf electric.

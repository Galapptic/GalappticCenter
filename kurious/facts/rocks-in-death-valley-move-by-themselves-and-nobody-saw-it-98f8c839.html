Rocks in Death Valley move by themselves and nobody saw it happen

For decades, massive boulders wandered across a dry lakebed, carving long tracks with zero witnesses to the crime.

On a cracked mud flat in Death Valley called Racetrack Playa, hundreds of rocks have somehow moved across the desert floor. Some weigh over 600 pounds. They leave perfectly visible trails behind them, some stretching for hundreds of feet. The truly weird part? For nearly a century, no human being ever actually saw them move. The rocks just appeared in new positions every few months or years, their paths etched in the dried mud like signatures from ghosts. Scientists were baffled. Tourists were enchanted. The stones kept their secret.

Rogue waves appear from nowhere and tower 100 feet high

For centuries, sailors described walls of water that appeared without warning. Everyone thought they were lying.

Imagine sailing across calm seas when suddenly a wall of water twice the height of surrounding waves rises up before you. This is a rogue wave, and until 1995, scientists dismissed them as maritime folklore. Sailors reported these monsters for generations, describing waves that towered over their ships without warning. Then on January 1st, 1995, a laser detector on an oil platform in the North Sea measured a wave 84 feet tall rising from seas with 40 foot waves. The Draupner wave proved sailors had been right all along. These oceanic giants are real, terrifying, and far more common than anyone imagined.

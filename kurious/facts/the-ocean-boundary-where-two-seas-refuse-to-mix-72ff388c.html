The Ocean Boundary Where Two Seas Refuse to Mix

In the Gulf of Alaska, a visible line stretches across the water where two bodies of ocean meet but stubbornly keep to themselves.

The photograph went viral in 2013: a sharp, undulating line cutting through open water, dark blue on one side, pale turquoise on the other. It looked like Photoshop, or perhaps divine intervention. It was neither. Researcher Ken Bruland, a marine chemist at UC Santa Cruz, captured the image during an expedition in the Gulf of Alaska. What he photographed was a natural phenomenon that occurs wherever glacial meltwater meets the open sea.

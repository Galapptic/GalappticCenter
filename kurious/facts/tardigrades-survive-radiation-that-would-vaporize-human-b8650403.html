Tardigrades Survive Radiation That Would Vaporize Human Cells

These microscopic creatures can withstand radiation doses 1,000 times higher than what would kill a human, emerging completely unharmed.

Tardigrades, affectionately called water bears or moss piglets, are eight-legged microscopic animals measuring just half a millimeter long. Despite their adorable appearance under a microscope, they're arguably Earth's most resilient creatures. While a radiation dose of 5 grays can kill a human, tardigrades shrug off doses exceeding 5,000 grays. At levels that would literally destroy human cells and DNA, these tiny extremophiles keep plodding along. They've been found everywhere from Himalayan peaks to ocean trenches, from Antarctic ice to your backyard moss. Their superpower isn't strength or speed—it's an almost supernatural ability to survive conditions that would obliterate virtually any other animal on Earth.

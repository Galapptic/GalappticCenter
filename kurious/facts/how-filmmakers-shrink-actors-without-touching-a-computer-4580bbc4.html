How Filmmakers Shrink Actors Without Touching a Computer

The hobbits in Lord of the Rings weren't digital creations—they were regular-sized actors standing much farther from the camera than you'd ever guess.

Your brain assumes that objects in the same frame occupy the same space. Filmmakers have exploited this quirk for over a century, using forced perspective to make actors appear giant or tiny without any post-production trickery. The technique is elegantly simple: place one actor close to the camera and another far behind, then compose the shot so both seem to stand side by side.

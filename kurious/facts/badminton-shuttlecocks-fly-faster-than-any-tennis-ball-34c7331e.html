Badminton Shuttlecocks Fly Faster Than Any Tennis Ball

The delicate feathered projectile that looks like it could barely survive a breeze actually smashes speed records at over 300 mph.

It seems counterintuitive, but that fragile-looking shuttlecock absolutely destroys tennis balls in speed comparisons. The fastest recorded badminton smash hit 306 mph, set by Malaysian player Tan Boon Heong. Meanwhile, the fastest tennis serve ever recorded was 163 mph by Sam Groth. That's nearly double the speed from a sport most people consider gentler than tennis. The secret lies in the shuttlecock's unique construction: sixteen goose feathers arranged in a cone, attached to a cork base. This design allows it to accelerate explosively off the racquet while the lightweight construction means less mass needs to be moved. Don't let its delicate appearance fool you—badminton is the fastest racquet sport on Earth.

The Princess Who Escaped Rape and Mutilation as a Nightingale

Philomela had her tongue cut out to silence her, but the gods gave her an eternal voice that still sings every night.

In Greek mythology, transformation wasn't always a punishment. Sometimes it was the only escape. Philomela, an Athenian princess, endured one of the most brutal stories in ancient literature—rape, mutilation, imprisonment—before the gods turned her into a nightingale. The bird's famous song, which has inspired poets from Ovid to T.S. Eliot, was said to be her voice finally set free. What she couldn't speak as a woman, she sang forever as a bird.

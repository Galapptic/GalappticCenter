Our Nearest Stellar Neighbor May Harbor Habitable Worlds

Just 4.2 light-years away, the closest star to our Sun hosts at least two planets—and one sits squarely in the zone where liquid water could exist.

Proxima Centauri, a dim red dwarf star, is the closest star to our solar system at just 4.2 light-years away. In 2016, astronomers discovered Proxima b, a rocky planet orbiting within its star's habitable zone—the region where temperatures could allow liquid water to exist on the surface. This planet is roughly Earth-sized, about 1.3 times our planet's mass. A second planet, Proxima c, was confirmed in 2020, orbiting much farther out. The discovery electrified scientists because it means our nearest stellar neighbor isn't just a lonely star—it's a potential host for alien life, making it a prime target for future interstellar exploration and observation.

Old Faithful Runs on Time Thanks to Underground Pipes

Yellowstone's most famous geyser erupts with clockwork regularity not because of magic, but because of a hidden plumbing system that scientists have finally mapped.

Old Faithful earned its name in 1870 because it erupted so predictably—roughly every 90 minutes, give or take. For over a century, nobody knew why this geyser kept such reliable time while others nearby erupted randomly. The answer, it turns out, lies in the pipes.

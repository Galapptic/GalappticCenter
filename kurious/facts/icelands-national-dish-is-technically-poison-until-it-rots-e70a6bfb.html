Iceland's National Dish Is Technically Poison Until It Rots

Greenland shark meat is so toxic it can make you drunk and delirious—unless you bury it underground for months and let bacteria do the work.

Fresh Greenland shark will make you violently ill. The fish has no kidneys or urinary tract, so urea and trimethylamine oxide build up in its flesh—compounds that break down into something chemically similar to being blackout drunk. Vikings discovered that if you can't cook the poison out, you can rot it out.

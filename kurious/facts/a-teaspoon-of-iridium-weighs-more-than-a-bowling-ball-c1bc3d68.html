A Teaspoon of Iridium Weighs More Than a Bowling Ball

The second-densest element on Earth packs so much mass into so little space that a sugar-cube-sized chunk would strain your wrist.

Iridium doesn't look like much—a silvery-white metal with a faint yellowish tinge. But pick up a piece and your brain short-circuits. It's so heavy it feels fake, like a magic trick. A single teaspoon of solid iridium weighs about 113 grams. A regulation bowling ball starts at just under 2.7 kilograms—but spread that weight across a sphere the size of a cantaloupe, and it feels almost airy compared to iridium's concentrated heft.

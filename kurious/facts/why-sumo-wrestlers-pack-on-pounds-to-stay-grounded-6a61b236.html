Why Sumo Wrestlers Pack on Pounds to Stay Grounded

The heavier a sumo wrestler gets, the harder physics makes it to knock them over—and they've understood this for centuries.

In most sports, athletes chase leanness. Sprinters strip away excess mass. Swimmers shave their bodies. But sumo wrestlers do the opposite: they deliberately pack on as much weight as possible, sometimes eating 10,000 calories a day. This isn't simply about building muscle or intimidating opponents. It's applied physics, refined over 1,500 years of Japanese tradition.

Your pupils betray your love before you even know it

The black circles at the center of your eyes expand involuntarily when you look at someone you're attracted to.

Your pupils are terrible liars. Those dark circles in the center of your eyes constantly change size, and you have absolutely no conscious control over them. When you see someone you find attractive or love, your pupils dilate noticeably, sometimes expanding by nearly a millimeter. This happens whether you want it to or not. It's one of the few truly honest signals your body sends, impossible to fake or suppress. People have been noticing this phenomenon for centuries. Women in Renaissance Italy even used belladonna drops to artificially dilate their pupils, believing it made them more beautiful and alluring to potential suitors.

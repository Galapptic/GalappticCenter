Badminton Shuttlecocks Outrace Tennis Balls by Over 100 MPH

The humble shuttlecock holds the record for the fastest struck object in any racquet sport, leaving tennis serves looking sluggish by comparison.

When Malaysian player Tan Boon Heong smashed a shuttlecock at 493 kilometers per hour in 2013, he created what remains one of sport's most jaw-dropping statistics. Compare this to the fastest tennis serve ever recorded—Samuel Groth's 263 km/h rocket in 2012. That's nearly double the speed from a sport most people consider leisurely and recreational. Professional badminton players routinely generate smashes exceeding 400 km/h during competitive play. The shuttlecock's initial velocity off the racquet makes it the fastest moving object in any racquet sport, outpacing not just tennis but also squash and table tennis. This counterintuitive fact surprises most sports fans who associate badminton with backyard barbecues rather than blistering speed.

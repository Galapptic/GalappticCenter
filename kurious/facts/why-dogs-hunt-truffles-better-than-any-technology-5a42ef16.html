Why Dogs Hunt Truffles Better Than Any Technology

A single white truffle can sell for $4,000 per pound, yet the only reliable way to find them is a dog's nose.

Truffles grow in total darkness, a few inches underground, attached to the roots of oak and hazelnut trees. They produce no visible sign on the surface. No flower, no bump, no discoloration. Yet trained dogs can locate them from twenty feet away, in forests where humans see nothing but dirt and leaves.

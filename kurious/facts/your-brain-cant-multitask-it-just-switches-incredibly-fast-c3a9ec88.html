Your brain can't multitask, it just switches incredibly fast

That feeling of doing two things at once? It's your brain pulling off an elaborate magic trick.

When you think you're texting while watching TV, your brain isn't actually doing both at once. It's rapidly toggling between them, giving each task a tiny slice of attention before jumping to the other. This switching happens so fast that it creates the illusion of simultaneity. Your conscious experience feels smooth and unified, but under the hood, your brain is more like a juggler keeping balls in the air than a computer running parallel processes. The switch occurs in milliseconds, which is why you never notice the gap. But the gap is always there.

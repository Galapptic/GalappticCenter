The Ancient Riddle That Makes You Question What You Are

A 2,000-year-old thought experiment about a rotting ship still stumps philosophers trying to define what makes you *you*.

The Athenians kept the ship of the hero Theseus in their harbor at Piraeus for centuries, replacing each plank as it decayed. By the time the Greek biographer Plutarch wrote about it around 75 CE, every original timber was gone. So was it still the ship of Theseus—or something else entirely?

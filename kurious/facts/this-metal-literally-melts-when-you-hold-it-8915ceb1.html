This Metal Literally Melts When You Hold It

Gallium transforms from solid metal to liquid puddle using nothing but the warmth of your palm.

Pick up a chunk of gallium, and within seconds, you'll feel it softening, drooping, and eventually pooling into a silvery liquid right in your hand. That's because gallium's melting point is just 29.76°C (85.6°F)—comfortably below the 37°C of human body temperature. It looks like aluminum, feels surprisingly heavy, and behaves like no other metal you've encountered. The experience is genuinely surreal: watching solid metal surrender to your body heat feels like a violation of everything you know about materials. It's completely safe to handle briefly, though you'll want to wash your hands afterward—gallium leaves a slight residue and can stain skin gray.

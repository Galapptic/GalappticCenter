Strangers in a Crowd Build Traffic Lanes Without Saying a Word

Put two streams of strangers on a sidewalk heading opposite ways, and within seconds they sort themselves into neat lanes that no one planned, chose or even noticed.

Stand on a busy sidewalk and watch closely. People walking north drift into one band, people walking south into another, and the two streams slide past each other with surprisingly few collisions. Nobody painted a line or gave an order. The lanes simply appear, and physicists have spent decades figuring out why.

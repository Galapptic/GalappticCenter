This Entire Country Exists Inside Another Country

Lesotho is one of only three countries on Earth completely surrounded by a single nation, yet it has maintained its independence for over 150 years.

Look at a map of southern Africa, and you'll spot something unusual: a country-shaped hole inside South Africa. That's Lesotho, a mountainous nation about the size of Belgium, entirely encircled by its much larger neighbor. Every border Lesotho has connects to South Africa—no other country touches it. This makes Lesotho an 'enclaved country,' completely surrounded by a single nation. Despite being landlocked within a landlocked situation, Lesotho maintains full independence with its own government, currency, and even a king. The country's survival as a sovereign state while being geographically swallowed by South Africa is one of modern geography's most fascinating political puzzles.

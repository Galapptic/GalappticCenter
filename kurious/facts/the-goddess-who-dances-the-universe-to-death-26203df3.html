The Goddess Who Dances the Universe to Death

In Hindu mythology, the world ends not with fire or flood, but with a goddess dancing on her husband's corpse until reality itself dissolves.

Kali is terrifying by design. She's depicted with black or blue skin, a necklace of severed heads, a skirt of human arms, and a lolling tongue dripping blood. But in Hindu tradition, she's not evil—she's the goddess who does the universe's necessary dirty work, destroying what must end so something new can begin.

The Colorblind Octopus That Perfectly Mimics Dozens of Species

A creature that cannot see colors somehow produces flawless impersonations of lionfish, flatfish, and sea snakes with zero visual feedback.

In the shallow waters of Indonesia, the mimic octopus performs something seemingly impossible. It transforms itself into lionfish, flatfish, sea snakes, and over a dozen other species with stunning accuracy. It doesn't just change color—it alters its body shape, movement patterns, and even swimming style to match each target. Yet here's the twist: like all octopuses, it's completely colorblind. Its eyes contain only one type of photoreceptor, meaning it sees the world in grayscale. Somehow, this master impressionist creates perfect color matches without ever seeing the colors it's producing. It chooses the right disguise for each threat, becoming a venomous lionfish when approached by damselfish, or a toxic sole when threatened by territorial fish.

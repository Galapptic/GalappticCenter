The Giant Wolf Chained by the Gods Until Doomsday

The Norse gods knew exactly how the world would end—a monstrous wolf they raised themselves would break free and swallow the sun, the moon, and Odin himself.

In most mythologies, the apocalypse comes as a surprise. Not in Norse tradition. The gods of Asgard received detailed prophecies about their own destruction: which monsters would rise, which gods would fall, and in what order the cosmos would collapse. At the center of this foretold catastrophe stood Fenrir, a wolf so vast that when he opened his jaws, one reached the sky and the other touched the earth. The gods raised him. They fed him. And they knew, from the moment of his birth, that he would eventually kill them all.

Two Latin Words Can Unlock Any Prison Door

A legal phrase invented 800 years ago remains the most powerful weapon against unlawful imprisonment anywhere in the world.

Habeas corpus literally translates to 'you shall have the body.' It's a legal command that forces authorities to bring a prisoner before a judge and prove they have a valid reason to hold them. No explanation? The prisoner walks free. This deceptively simple concept is arguably the most important legal protection ever created. It prevents governments from secretly detaining people, holding them indefinitely without charges, or making them disappear into dungeons. When you file a habeas corpus petition, you're essentially demanding the state justify its power over you. If they can't, their power evaporates. Two words, written in a dead language, standing between you and tyranny.

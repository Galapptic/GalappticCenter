When Banks Pay You to Borrow Money

In 2019, a Danish bank offered mortgages at negative 0.5% interest—meaning the bank paid borrowers to take out home loans.

Jyske Bank made headlines in August 2019 when it launched the world's first negative-interest mortgage. Borrow money to buy a house, and the bank would actually reduce your principal each month. The catch? This wasn't generosity—it was survival.

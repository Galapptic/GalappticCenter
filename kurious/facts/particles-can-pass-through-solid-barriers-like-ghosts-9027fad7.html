Particles Can Pass Through Solid Barriers Like Ghosts

The same quantum weirdness that lets particles teleport through walls is happening inside your body right now, powering every heartbeat.

Imagine throwing a tennis ball at a brick wall and watching it appear on the other side without breaking anything. Absurd, right? Yet particles do exactly this constantly. Quantum tunneling allows electrons, protons, and other tiny particles to pass through barriers they shouldn't have enough energy to overcome. It's not that they smash through or find holes—they genuinely appear on the other side as if the barrier barely exists. This isn't science fiction; it's fundamental physics happening everywhere. The Sun shines because of tunneling. Your phone works because of tunneling. Even some biological processes depend on it. At the quantum scale, particles don't follow our everyday rules about walls and boundaries.

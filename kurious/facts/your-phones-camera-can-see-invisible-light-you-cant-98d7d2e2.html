Your phone's camera can see invisible light you can't

Point any TV remote at your phone's camera and press a button to see something your eyes never could.

Your smartphone camera has a superpower you've probably never noticed. It can see infrared light, the invisible radiation just beyond the red end of the spectrum. Try it right now. Open your camera app, point a TV remote at the lens, and press any button. You'll see a bright flash on your screen that's completely invisible to your naked eye. That's infrared light pouring out of the remote's LED. Your eyes can't detect it, but your phone's camera sensor absolutely can. The manufacturers actually work hard to prevent this ability, but they can never block it completely.

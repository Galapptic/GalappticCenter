Ancient Sculptors Left Fingerprints We Can Still Read Today

The whorls and ridges pressed into 2,500-year-old clay are so clear that forensic experts could match them to the artisans who shaped Greek masterpieces.

When Greek sculptors smoothed wet clay into gods and athletes, they left behind more than art. They left themselves—literally pressed into the surface. Fingerprints, palm prints, even the faint texture of skin on knuckles survive on terracotta figurines and clay models that are thousands of years old.

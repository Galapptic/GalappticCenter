This Entire Language Has Fewer Words Than a Toddler Knows

While English has over 170,000 words, one fully functional language expresses complex philosophy, poetry, and daily life with just 120.

Toki Pona is the world's smallest language, created in 2001 by Canadian linguist Sonja Lang. With only around 120 words, speakers combine simple concepts to express complex ideas. "Car" becomes "tomo tawa" (moving room). "Friend" is "jan pona" (good person). The language forces you to break down thoughts into their fundamental components. Lang designed it as an experiment in minimalism, partly inspired by Taoist philosophy and the idea that simplifying language might simplify thought itself. Despite its tiny vocabulary, Toki Pona has thousands of speakers worldwide, complete translations of major texts, and an active online community proving you don't need many words to communicate deeply.

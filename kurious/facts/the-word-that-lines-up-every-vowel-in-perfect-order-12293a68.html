The Word That Lines Up Every Vowel in Perfect Order

Only one common English word contains all five vowels exactly once, in alphabetical order—and it's hiding in plain sight.

Say "unpaired" slowly. U-N-P-A-I-R-E-D. The consonants do their usual work, but the vowels—A, E, I—slip through in perfect alphabetical sequence, each appearing exactly once. It's the only common English word that pulls off this trick with just the essential three vowels (A, I, and E appearing in that strict order through the word's structure).

Your DNA Carries Traces of a Ghost Species

A finger bone found in a Siberian cave revealed that modern humans carry genetic gifts from a mysterious cousin we've never seen face-to-face.

In 2010, scientists sequenced DNA from a 40,000-year-old pinky bone discovered in Denisova Cave, Russia. The genetic code didn't match Neanderthals or modern humans. It belonged to an entirely unknown group of ancient humans, now called Denisovans. We have almost no fossils—just a few teeth, bone fragments, and that single finger—yet their DNA lives on in billions of people today.

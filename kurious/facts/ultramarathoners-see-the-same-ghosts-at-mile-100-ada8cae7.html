Ultramarathoners See the Same Ghosts at Mile 100

Runners separated by hours on remote trails independently report seeing identical phantom figures, shadow people, and mysterious companions that vanish when approached.

Something strange happens when humans push beyond 100 miles on foot. Ultramarathon runners across different races, countries, and years report eerily similar hallucinations: shadow figures darting between trees, phantom runners keeping pace beside them, and mysterious dark shapes that dissolve upon closer inspection. These aren't random fever dreams. Runners who've never met describe nearly identical visions—hooded figures, silent companions, animals that couldn't possibly be there. Race directors have documented these reports for decades, noting the consistency is far too precise for coincidence. The exhausted brain, pushed to its absolute limit, appears to default to the same hallucinatory templates regardless of the individual experiencing them.

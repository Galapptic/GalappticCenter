A Spider Outsmarted the Sky God to Own All Stories

Every folktale you've ever heard technically belongs to a tiny spider who conned the most powerful god in West African mythology.

Anansi is no ordinary spider. In Akan mythology from Ghana, this eight-legged trickster pulled off the greatest heist in folklore history: he stole all the world's stories from Nyame, the sky god himself. Nyame had hoarded every tale ever told, keeping them locked away in the heavens. Anansi wanted them for humanity—and for the glory of having his name attached to every story forever after. The sky god laughed at the tiny spider's request and set him seemingly impossible tasks. But Anansi wasn't just clever; he was cunningly manipulative, using psychology rather than strength to achieve what warriors and kings could not.

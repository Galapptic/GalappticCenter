Medieval Monks Forged Documents That Still Shape Property Law Today

Some of the most brazen lies in European history were written by monks who fabricated thousands of royal charters, land grants, and papal decrees—and many weren't exposed for 700 years.

In 1440, Italian humanist Lorenzo Valla proved that the Donation of Constantine—a document claiming Emperor Constantine had granted the Pope authority over the Western Roman Empire—was an eighth-century forgery. The Latin was wrong for Constantine's era. The text referenced institutions that didn't exist yet. For 600 years, this fake had justified papal territorial claims across Europe.

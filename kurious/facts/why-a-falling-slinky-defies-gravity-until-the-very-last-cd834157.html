Why a Falling Slinky Defies Gravity Until the Very Last Second

Drop a stretched slinky and its bottom stays frozen in midair—seemingly ignoring gravity—until the top crashes down to meet it.

In 2011, physicist Rod Cross at the University of Sydney filmed something that looked impossible. He held a slinky by its top, let it dangle fully extended, then released it. The top began falling immediately. The bottom? It hung motionless in space, levitating for a fraction of a second as if gravity had simply forgotten about it.

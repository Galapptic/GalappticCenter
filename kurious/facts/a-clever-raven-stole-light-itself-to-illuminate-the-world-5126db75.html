A Clever Raven Stole Light Itself to Illuminate the World

In Pacific Northwest mythology, the entire cosmos was pitch black until a shape-shifting bird pulled off the greatest heist in existence.

Long before humans walked the earth, darkness covered everything. The Raven, one of the most important figures in Pacific Northwest Indigenous mythology, discovered that a powerful chief kept the sun, moon, and stars locked away in boxes. Using his shape-shifting abilities, Raven transformed himself into a pine needle and was swallowed by the chief's daughter when she drank water. She gave birth to a strange child who was actually Raven in disguise. The boy cried constantly until his doting grandfather let him play with the precious boxes. One by one, Raven released the stars, then the moon, and finally burst open the box containing the sun, flooding the world with light and escaping through the smokehole.

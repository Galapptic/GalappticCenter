The Metal Too Heavy to Lift with a Spoon

A teaspoon of osmium weighs about the same as a bowling ball, making it the densest naturally occurring element on Earth.

Pick up a teaspoon. Now imagine it filled with a bluish-silver metal so impossibly dense that it weighs roughly 4.5 kilograms—about as much as a standard bowling ball. This isn't a thought experiment. The metal is osmium, element 76 on the periodic table, and it holds the title for the densest naturally occurring substance on Earth.

Your brain invents words you never actually heard

That crystal clear conversation you just had? Parts of it were hallucinated by your brain.

Listen carefully to someone speaking in a noisy café. You hear every word perfectly, right? Wrong. Your brain is lying to you. Scientists discovered this by recording sentences and deleting specific sounds, replacing them with coughs or static. When people listen to these doctored recordings, they swear they heard the missing phonemes clearly. They don't perceive gaps or notice anything strange. Their brains simply fabricate the absent sounds, filling in blanks so seamlessly that the listener experiences a complete, unbroken sentence. This phenomenon is called phonemic restoration, and it happens constantly during everyday conversation without you ever knowing.

This octopus perfectly impersonates 15 different animals on command

One moment it's a flatfish, the next a lionfish, then a sea snake—all the same octopus.

The mimic octopus does something no other animal can do. It doesn't just change color or texture like other octopuses. It actually transforms its entire body to look and move like completely different sea creatures. Scientists have watched it impersonate at least fifteen different species, from flatfish to lionfish to sea snakes. When a predator approaches, it assesses the threat and chooses which animal to become. If a damselfish threatens it, the octopus might flatten itself and glide along the seafloor like a sole. If something else attacks, it raises its arms to mimic the venomous spines of a lionfish. It's deliberate, strategic theater.

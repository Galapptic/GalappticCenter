This Metal Literally Melts in the Palm of Your Hand

Gallium looks like solid steel until you hold it, when it transforms into a silvery puddle right before your eyes.

Pick up a chunk of gallium and something almost magical happens. This silvery metal, which looks perfectly solid and metallic sitting on a table, begins to liquefy the moment it touches your skin. That's because gallium's melting point is just 29.76°C (85.6°F), comfortably below human body temperature of 37°C. Within minutes, what was a solid metallic chunk becomes a shimmering pool of liquid metal in your palm. Unlike mercury, gallium is non-toxic, so this party trick is actually safe to perform. The metal resolidifies when you set it down, ready to melt again whenever someone picks it up.

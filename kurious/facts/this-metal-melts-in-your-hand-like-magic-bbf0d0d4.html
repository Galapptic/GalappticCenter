This Metal Melts in Your Hand Like Magic

Gallium is a metal so sensitive to warmth that simply holding it transforms solid metal into a shimmering puddle on your palm.

Gallium looks like aluminum's shinier cousin—silvery, metallic, and solid at room temperature. But pick it up, and something bizarre happens. With a melting point of just 29.76°C (85.6°F), gallium liquefies from your body heat alone. You can literally watch a solid chunk of metal dissolve into a mirror-like liquid pool in your hand. Unlike mercury, gallium isn't toxic to touch, making it a favorite for chemistry demonstrations and viral videos. The experience is surreal—metal isn't supposed to behave this way. It's like holding a piece of the Terminator that's giving up. This unusual property places gallium among the handful of elements that exist near the liquid-solid boundary at everyday temperatures.

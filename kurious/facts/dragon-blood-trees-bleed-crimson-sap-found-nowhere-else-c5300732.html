Dragon Blood Trees Bleed Crimson Sap Found Nowhere Else

On a remote Yemeni island so alien it's called the 'Galapagos of the Indian Ocean,' trees weep blood-red resin that humans have treasured for over two thousand years.

Rising from the Arabian Sea off Yemen's coast, Socotra Island hosts one of Earth's most surreal landscapes. The Dragon Blood Tree looks like something from science fiction—its umbrella-shaped crown evolved to capture morning mist in this arid environment. But the real magic lies within. When cut, these trees bleed a deep crimson resin called dragon's blood. This isn't just visually striking; the compound is chemically unique to Socotra. Over a third of the island's plant species exist nowhere else on Earth, making it a living laboratory of evolution. The Dragon Blood Tree has become Socotra's iconic symbol, representing millions of years of isolated evolution.

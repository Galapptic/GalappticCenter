Your car key starts the engine by asking secret mathematical questions

Every time you press that button, your key and car exchange cryptographic riddles in milliseconds.

When you press your car key, you're not just sending a signal. Your key and your car are having a conversation, and it's one that eavesdroppers can't join. Modern keyless entry systems use something called challenge-response authentication. Think of it like a secret handshake that changes every single time. Your car asks a question that only your real key can answer correctly. The question is different each time, so recording the answer won't help a thief. This happens so fast you never notice the mathematical dance occurring in the air between your pocket and your dashboard.

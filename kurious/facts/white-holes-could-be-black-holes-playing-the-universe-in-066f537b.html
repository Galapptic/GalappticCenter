White Holes Could Be Black Holes Playing the Universe in Reverse

Einstein's equations predict cosmic objects that vomit matter instead of swallowing it, running backward through time itself.

Imagine a black hole running in reverse. Instead of pulling everything in, it violently pushes everything out. That's a white hole—a theoretical exit door in spacetime where matter and light can only escape, never enter. The mathematics of general relativity permits these strange objects to exist. If black holes are cosmic drains, white holes are fountains, eternally gushing energy and matter into the universe. Nothing can fall into a white hole no matter how hard it tries. The same equations Einstein wrote that predict black holes also predict these time-reversed counterparts. They're not science fiction—they're mathematically valid solutions hiding in our best theory of gravity.

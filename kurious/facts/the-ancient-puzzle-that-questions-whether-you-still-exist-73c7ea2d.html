The Ancient Puzzle That Questions Whether You Still Exist

A Greek historian's story about rotting planks has tormented philosophers for 2,400 years—and it's about to get personal.

Plutarch, writing around 75 CE, recorded a puzzle that Athenians had been arguing about for centuries. The ship that carried Theseus back from Crete after slaying the Minotaur was preserved in the harbor at Piraeus as a sacred relic. But wood rots. So the Athenians replaced each decaying plank with fresh timber, one by one, until eventually no original material remained. The philosophers of Athens couldn't agree: was this still the ship of Theseus, or something else entirely?

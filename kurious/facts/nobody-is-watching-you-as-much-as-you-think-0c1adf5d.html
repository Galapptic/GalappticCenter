Nobody Is Watching You as Much as You Think

That embarrassing moment you've been replaying in your head for years? Most people forgot it within seconds.

You walk into a meeting late, convinced every eye is judging you. You notice a stain on your shirt and assume everyone else has too. This is the spotlight effect—our tendency to drastically overestimate how much others notice our appearance, actions, and mistakes. The truth? Most people are too busy worrying about themselves to scrutinize you. We're each the star of our own mental movie, so we naturally assume we're prominent characters in everyone else's story too. But here's the liberating reality: you're barely an extra in most people's daily narratives. That awkward thing you said? They've already forgotten while you're still cringing at 2 AM.

The Element That Makes Your Breath Smell Like Garlic for Weeks

A speck of tellurium so small it's invisible can transform your breath into an unshakeable garlic stench that lingers for months.

In the late 19th century, gold miners in Colorado started noticing something strange. Workers at certain mines developed a powerful garlic odor that no amount of bathing could remove. Their breath reeked. Their sweat reeked. The smell followed them home, seeped into their clothes, and lingered for weeks after they left the mines. The culprit wasn't poor hygiene or an unusual diet. It was tellurium, a rare metalloid element that the human nose can detect in quantities so small they border on the absurd.

Your Seat at the Meeting Table Reveals More Than You Think

Without anyone saying a word, the chairs people choose in a conference room quietly sort them into a pecking order that can last for years.

No one assigns seats. There's no memo, no name cards. Yet within minutes of a team's first meeting, people settle into positions that telegraph who matters—and these arrangements tend to stick. Researchers have found that where someone sits predicts how much they speak, how often they're interrupted, and how influential others perceive them to be.

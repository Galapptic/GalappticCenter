About
A happy little helper for potty training. A potty timer reminds you when it’s time to try, all day, even with the app closed, and starts over after every trip. Every pee or poop in the potty starts a party: confetti, a buddy who jumps for joy, a cheer that says your child’s name, and a sticker your child picks for their chart. Fill the chart to win the prize you chose and meet a new buddy. See when your little one usually needs to go, watch accidents fade, celebrate every first, and find warm, practical guides for every bump in the road. No account, no ads, no tracking: everything stays on your phone.

About
A gentle, private companion for quitting kratom and 7-OH. Tell Unleaf what you take and how much, pick a pace, and get a daily limit that steps down every few days to a clear jump day, with smaller steps at the end and the freedom to hold any step when it gets hard. Log doses in seconds, check in each evening, ride out cravings with SOS, and once you stop, see what withdrawal usually feels like hour by hour and day by day. Every good day grows a little plant that never shrinks. No account, no ads, no tracking: everything stays on your phone.

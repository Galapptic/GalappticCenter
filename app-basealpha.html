Base Alpha
Build a base on the Moon. Make real discoveries.

🍎 App Store — Coming Soon
About
A 3D strategy game about building the first science base on the Moon. Pick one of five real landing sites, set down your habitat, and build the power, oxygen, water and food your crew need before the Sun sets on a night that lasts two weeks. Then do what you came for: survey strange sites, record moonquakes and solar flares, and study what you find to make discoveries drawn from real lunar science, from ancient comet ice to the radio glow of the universe before the first stars. Every discovery tells you what it means for people on Earth. No ads, no purchases, no account, and it plays fully offline on iPhone and iPad.

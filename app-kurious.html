About
Kurious serves up something new to learn every day: 7 real facts, each from a different category, drawn from 21 that rotate, from chemistry and space to mythology and law. Each fact is a short illustrated article that reads in one smooth scroll, with real photos and credited sources, from quick reads to longer deep dives. New facts are written fresh each night and are ready at midnight Central Time, and a subject never repeats within a month. Built for curious people who want to learn something genuinely fascinating every day, without the doom-scroll.

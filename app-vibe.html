About
A pass-and-play party game for groups of 2 to 15. One player is the Guesser and everyone else sees a secret number between 1 and 10. The Guesser gives each person a category and tries to figure out the number from their answers. 300+ curated categories, two game modes, and a fully custom visual identity.
